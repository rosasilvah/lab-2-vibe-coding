About

I am a second-year Master’s student in Dr. Su’s lab, working on force sensing for neurovascular robots in stroke intervention. Previously, I contributed to the DeSimone Lab at Stanford University, optimizing 3D-printed microneedle patches. I have also worked in the Jurney Lab and Bellofiore Lab at SJSU.
